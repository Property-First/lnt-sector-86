L&T Realty Sector 86 Gurgaon Project Overview

L&T Realty Sector 86 Gurgaon is an upcoming luxury residential development by L&T Realty, located in Sector 86, Gurugram. The project is planned across approximately 20 acres and features spacious 3 & 4 BHK high-rise residences in New Gurugram.

The development has an estimated potential of approximately 3.6 million sq. ft. and is planned as a large residential community with landscaped open spaces, lifestyle amenities and contemporary high-rise homes designed for comfortable urban living.

Located in New Gurugram, the project offers access to major road networks including Dwarka Expressway, NH-48 and Central Peripheral Road (CPR). The surrounding area also provides connectivity to schools, healthcare facilities, shopping destinations and major commercial hubs.
